## Get Up and Running

Follow these four steps to go from sign-up to a fully operational RBOS workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Workspace">
    Sign up at [app.rbos.io](https://app.rbos.io) and provision a new workspace for your organization. Choose a workspace name that reflects your team or company, and select the plan that fits your needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set Up Your First Project">
    Inside your workspace, click **+ New Project** to create a structured container for your team's work. Add a name, description, and tags to keep things organized from the start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Documents">
    Upload files directly via drag-and-drop, or use the **Import** button to pull in content from Google Drive, Notion, GitHub, and other connected sources. All documents become instantly searchable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy an AI Agent">
    Navigate to the **Agents** section, click **+ New Agent**, and select a template such as "Knowledge Assistant." Connect the agent to your project and start asking questions against your own content.
  </Step>
</Steps>
