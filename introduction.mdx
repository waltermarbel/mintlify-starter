Most organizations run on a patchwork of disconnected tools — documents scattered across drives, projects tracked in separate apps, and AI capabilities bolted on as an afterthought. RBOS replaces that fragmentation with a single, governed platform where your knowledge, work, and AI agents operate together in one coherent system.

## What Is RBOS?

RBOS (Role-Based Operating System) is an enterprise knowledge-base operating system designed to give organizations the same kind of structured, unified environment for knowledge work that a traditional OS gives a single computer. Where conventional tools like wikis, project trackers, and document stores operate in silos, RBOS treats every document, task, workflow, and AI agent as a first-class citizen in a shared, searchable, permissioned environment.

Unlike point solutions that solve one problem well, RBOS is built around the idea that knowledge, process, and intelligence belong together. When your documents live alongside your workflows and your AI agents have governed access to both, the entire organization moves faster and with greater confidence.

## Who Uses RBOS?

**Knowledge workers** use RBOS as their daily home for documents, project context, and research — replacing the constant context-switching between wikis, chat tools, and file storage.

**Team leads and project managers** rely on RBOS Projects to track goals, assign work, and connect tasks directly to the documents and workflows that support them, all in one view.

**Compliance officers and administrators** depend on RBOS Governance features — audit logs, role assignments, and retention policies — to ensure the organization meets regulatory requirements without slowing teams down.

**Developers and AI integrators** use RBOS's agent framework and integrations to build AI-powered workflows that connect internal knowledge to external tools like Slack, GitHub, Jira, and Google Drive.
