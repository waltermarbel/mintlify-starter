RBOS is built for enterprise environments where control, accountability, and compliance are non-negotiable. Every action taken in your workspace is governed by a layered permission model, recorded in tamper-proof audit logs, and subject to configurable data retention policies — so your team can move fast without sacrificing oversight.

## Security Features

In addition to role-based access control, RBOS provides several infrastructure-level security features to protect your workspace data.

<Accordion title="Single Sign-On (SSO)">
  RBOS supports **SAML 2.0** and **OIDC**-based SSO on the Enterprise plan. Once configured, members sign in through your identity provider (IdP), and group memberships can be mapped directly to RBOS workspace roles for automatic provisioning.

  To configure SSO, navigate to **Workspace Settings → Security → SSO**, choose your protocol, and supply your IdP metadata URL or certificate. See [Permissions](/governance/permissions) for the full setup walkthrough.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="IP Allowlist">
  Enterprise workspaces can restrict access to a defined list of IP ranges. Any login or API request originating from outside the allowlist is automatically blocked, regardless of valid credentials.

  To manage your allowlist, go to **Workspace Settings → Security → IP Allowlist** and add individual addresses or CIDR ranges.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Encryption">
  All data stored in RBOS is encrypted at rest using AES-256. All data transmitted between your browser or API client and RBOS servers is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption is enabled by default and cannot be disabled.
</Accordion>

## Getting Compliance Reports

You can access RBOS compliance documentation directly from your workspace or by contacting the support team.

- **SOC 2 Type II Report** — Available to customers on request. Contact [support@rbos.io](mailto:support@rbos.io) with your workspace name and a member of the security team will provide the report under NDA.
- **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** — Required for GDPR compliance. You can review and sign the DPA from **Workspace Settings → Security → Compliance Documents**.
- **HIPAA BAA** — Enterprise customers requiring a Business Associate Agreement should contact their account manager or reach out to [enterprise@rbos.io](mailto:enterprise@rbos.io).

<Note>
  Data residency options (EU and US regions) must be selected at workspace creation time. Migrating an existing workspace to a different region requires contacting Enterprise support.
</Note>
