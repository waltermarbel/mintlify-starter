This guide walks you through everything you need to go from a blank slate to a working RBOS environment. By the end, you'll have a live workspace with a project, documents loaded into your knowledge base, and an AI agent ready to answer questions against your content.

<Note>
  Trial workspaces include up to 5 members, 1 GB of document storage, and 3 AI agent deployments. Upgrade your plan at any time from **Settings → Billing** to remove these limits.
</Note>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Workspace">
    Navigate to [app.rbos.io](https://app.rbos.io) and click **Sign Up**. Enter your work email address and create a password, or continue with Google or Microsoft SSO.

    Once authenticated, you'll be prompted to create your first workspace:

    - **Workspace name** — Use your company or team name (e.g., `Acme Corp` or `Product Team`). This appears across the UI and in shared links.
    - **Plan** — Select a plan based on your team size and storage needs. You can start with the free trial and upgrade later.
    - **Slug** — RBOS auto-generates a URL slug from your workspace name (e.g., `acme-corp.rbos.io`). You can customize this before confirming.

    Click **Create Workspace** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite Your Team">
    A workspace is most powerful with your whole team inside it. Navigate to **Settings → Members** and click **Invite Members**.

    Enter one or more email addresses and assign each invitee a role before sending:

    - **Admin** — Full access to workspace settings, billing, integrations, and all content.
    - **Editor** — Can create, edit, and delete projects, documents, and workflows, but cannot change workspace settings.
    - **Viewer** — Read-only access to all content they've been granted visibility into.

    RBOS sends each invitee an email with a join link valid for 72 hours. You can resend or revoke invitations at any time from the **Members** panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create Your First Project">
    Projects are the primary way RBOS organizes work. Think of a project as a scoped container that holds tasks, documents, workflows, and the team members responsible for them — all oriented around a single goal.

    From your workspace home, click **+ New Project** in the left sidebar. Fill in the project details:

    - **Name** — A clear, descriptive title (e.g., `Q3 Product Launch` or `SOC 2 Compliance`).
    - **Description** — A brief summary of the project's purpose and scope.
    - **Tags** — Add tags to make the project discoverable via search and filters.

    Click **Create Project**. Your new project opens immediately, ready for documents and team members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Documents">
    With your project open, click the **Documents** tab and start populating your knowledge base.

    You have two options:

    - **Drag and drop** — Drag files directly from your desktop into the Documents panel. RBOS accepts PDF, DOCX, Markdown, plain text, and a range of other formats.
    - **Import** — Click the **Import** button to pull in content from a connected source. Supported integrations include Google Drive, Notion, GitHub, and Confluence. If you haven't connected an integration yet, go to **Settings → Integrations** first.

    All uploaded documents are automatically indexed for full-text and semantic search the moment they're added. Large files may take a few moments to finish indexing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch Your First AI Agent">
    Navigate to the **Agents** section in the left sidebar and click **+ New Agent**.

    RBOS offers several agent templates to get you started quickly:

    - **Knowledge Assistant** — Answers questions by searching across documents in your knowledge base.
    - **Project Summarizer** — Generates status summaries and action-item digests from project content.
    - **Workflow Runner** — Monitors triggers and executes workflow steps on your behalf.

    Select the **Knowledge Assistant** template and configure it:

    1. Give your agent a name (e.g., `Q3 Launch Assistant`).
    2. Under **Scope**, select the project or knowledge base folders the agent is allowed to access.
    3. Review the default permissions and adjust what actions the agent can take (read-only vs. read/write).
    4. Click **Deploy Agent**.

    Your agent is now live. Open it and ask a question — it will search your documents and respond with cited answers.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the quick search shortcut **Cmd+K** (macOS) or **Ctrl+K** (Windows/Linux) from anywhere in RBOS to instantly jump to any project, document, agent, or settings page. It's the fastest way to navigate once your workspace starts to grow.
</Tip>
